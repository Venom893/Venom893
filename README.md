🧭 Who am I?
I'm an Electrical Engineering graduate interested in the part of semiconductor design where ideas become real hardware.
My main interests sit at the intersection of:
🔬 Transistor-level circuit design
🧩 Analog & mixed-signal IC design
💻 RTL / digital design & verification
🏗️ ASIC / SoC implementation
📐 Synthesis → timing → physical design
⚙️ SPICE-based simulation & device behaviour
I enjoy going beyond "does it work?" and asking why it works, how it can be modeled, verified, synthesized and implemented.
