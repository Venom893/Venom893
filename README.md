### 🚀 Featured Projects

**[Low-Power 8-bit MAC Accelerator — RTL-to-GDSII (Sky130)](https://github.com/Venom893/Low-Power-MAC-Sky130--RTL-to-GDSII)**
Complete digital ASIC flow — synthesis, place-and-route, DRC, and LVS. 100 MHz timing closure, 0.864 mW, 5,062 µm² core, zero DRC violations, exact LVS device match (431/431).
`SystemVerilog` `Yosys` `OpenROAD` `Magic` `Netgen` `KLayout` `Docker`

**[GGNMOS ESD Protection Cell](https://github.com/Venom893/GGNMOS-ESD-Protection-Cadence)**
Gate-grounded NMOS ESD clamp on a 90nm PDK. Diagnosed a PDK compact-model limitation and built a custom Verilog-A behavioral macro-model, verified under 2kV HBM transient stress.
`Cadence Virtuoso` `Spectre` `Verilog-A` `SPICE`

**[Fault Detection in 13-Bus DG-Integrated Microgrid](https://github.com/Venom893/DG-Fault-Detection)**
Modeled and simulated symmetrical/unsymmetrical faults in a DG-integrated microgrid.
`MATLAB` `Simulink`
